# Cloudflare

- **URL:** https://www.cloudflare.com
- **Screenshots viewed:** `shots/cloudflare-hero.png`, `shots/cloudflare.png`

## One plain sentence
A single platform to build, secure, and scale your apps, AI agents, and internal tools without you having to run the servers yourself.

## The hero
- **Headline:** "Everything we learned from powering 20% of the Internet - yours by default"
- **Subhead:** "One platform for your apps, agents, and workforce. Build, secure, and scale without managing infrastructure."
- **CTA:** "Start building for free" (single white pill on solid orange field). A thin pill banner above promotes "Connect 2026 - The Agentic Conference of the Year."
- **Why it lands (partly):** The "20% of the Internet" number is a concrete, memorable proof point a layman can grasp - scale you can feel. "Yours by default" implies you inherit that power for free. But "apps, agents, and workforce" and "managing infrastructure" are still insider terms; a true non-technical reader would not know what "infrastructure" they are being spared.

## Landing structure (top to bottom, from full-page shot)
1. Hero (orange, headline + subhead + CTA).
2. "Region: Earth" - one smart network, close to users/data, with three benefit columns (Run everywhere / Run anywhere / Run at massive scale).
3. Social proof: "Cloudflare powers 45% of the Fortune 500" + "Trusted by the teams you trust."
4. "Why choose Cloudflare" - a comparison visual ("Fighting infra with cloud" vs "Shipping with Cloudflare").
5. "Pay only when your code runs" - pricing tiers (Free / Pro / Business / Contract) with plain feature lists.
6. "Tailored to your team" - fits existing workflows, fast path to AI adoption, secure by default.
7. Closing band: "Build without boundaries" + "Start building for free" (orange again).
8. Deep footer with three trust one-liners (DDoS defense, predictable pricing, Zero Trust VPN).

## Branding
- **Color:** Signature Cloudflare orange as a full-bleed hero and closing band; white body sections with black text. High-contrast, confident, unmistakably "theirs."
- **Typography:** Large geometric sans headline, tight leading, sentence case. Body is calm and readable.
- **Logo/mark:** Orange cloud glyph + "CLOUDFLARE" wordmark in tracked caps.
- **Tone:** Big, established, matter-of-fact. Leads with scale and safety, not hype.

## UX / patterns worth borrowing
- A single, concrete proof number in the headline ("20% of the Internet") instead of adjectives.
- "Region: Earth" gives an abstract network a simple mental image.
- Pricing framed as a benefit ("Pay only when your code runs") rather than a table dump.
- Bottom trust one-liners restate protections in plain terms.
- Exactly one primary CTA repeated top and bottom.

## What NOT to borrow
- "apps, agents, and workforce" plus "infrastructure" stacks jargon the target layman will not parse.
- The full-bleed saturated orange is brand equity Cloudflare has earned; a new product using one loud color everywhere can read as aggressive.
- Fortune 500 / logo-wall trust only works once you actually have those logos.
