# Mercury

- **URL:** https://mercury.com
- **Screenshots viewed:** `shots/mercury-hero.png`, `shots/mercury.png` (full-page shot only rendered the hero + footer; the middle lazy-loaded and did not capture)

## One plain sentence
Online banking built for startups and businesses that you can open and use in about ten minutes.

## The hero
- **Headline:** "Radically different banking"
- **Subhead:** "Apply online in 10 minutes to experience banking(1) unlike anything that's come before."
- **CTA:** Inline "Enter your email" field + "Open account" (primary blue pill) + "Launch demo" (secondary). A compliance disclaimer sits at the bottom of the hero: "Mercury is a fintech company, not an FDIC-insured bank. Banking services provided through Choice Financial Group and Column N.A., Members FDIC."
- **Why it lands:** Very layman-friendly. Everyone understands "banking," "apply in 10 minutes," and "open an account." The concrete time promise removes friction anxiety. The visible legal disclaimer right in the hero is honest trust-building, not buried in a footer.

## The hero image
A photoreal surreal scene: a leather office chair and wooden desk with a laptop, sitting on a grassy mountain ridge above a misty valley at dawn. It sells a feeling (calm, freedom, "work from anywhere") rather than explaining a feature - emotional, not functional.

## Landing structure
- Only the hero and the footer rendered in the capture. The footer shows a wide, organized column set (Banking, Company, Solutions, Resources, Product Notes) plus extended legal/disclaimer copy - typical of a regulated fintech. Middle sections did not load, so I cannot describe them.

## Branding
- **Color:** Soft, atmospheric gradient (dawn tones - warm tan into cool blue) behind the hero; single indigo/blue accent for the primary button; dark navy body below.
- **Typography:** Light-weight, wide serif-ish grotesque for the headline ("Radically different banking") - airy, premium, a little editorial.
- **Logo/mark:** Circular knot/orb glyph + "MERCURY" in tracked caps.
- **Tone:** Aspirational, serene, grown-up. Sells lifestyle and ease over spec.

## UX / patterns worth borrowing
- Concrete, low-friction promise in the subhead ("10 minutes").
- Email capture directly in the hero so the primary action needs no scroll.
- **The compliance disclaimer placed openly in the hero** - for a trust product, saying the honest caveat up front builds credibility. Directly relevant to babit as an "evidence/accountability" product.
- One aspirational image that conveys the feeling of the product instead of a UI screenshot.

## What NOT to borrow
- The dreamy mountain image communicates a mood but says nothing about what the product actually does - only works because "banking" is already understood. babit's category is not understood, so a purely emotional image would leave a layman lost.
- "Radically different" is a claim without evidence; it survives only because the 10-minute promise backs it.
