# Persona

- **URL:** https://withpersona.com
- **Screenshots viewed:** `shots/persona-hero.png`, `shots/persona.png`

## One plain sentence
A flexible service that lets online businesses confirm the people and companies they deal with are real.

## The hero
- **Headline:** "Keeping the internet human"
- **Subhead:** "Persona is the most flexible and secure way to verify real people and real businesses online."
- **CTAs:** "Get a demo" (black pill) + "See our full platform" (text link). A lavender announcement bar above the nav: "Persona is named a Leader in the 2026 Gartner Magic Quadrant for Identity Verification." Below the hero: a logo row (LinkedIn, OpenAI, Twilio, Etsy, Lyft).
- **Why it lands:** "Keeping the internet human" is a rare example of a plain-English, human-scale mission line for a deeply technical product (identity verification, KYC/KYB). Anyone worried about bots and fake accounts gets it instantly. The subhead then translates the poetry into a concrete function ("verify real people and real businesses"). Excellent layman handoff: emotional hook -> literal explanation.

## Landing structure (from full-page shot)
1. Hero (headline + subhead + CTAs).
2. Customer logo row (Fiverr, OpenAI, Twilio, Etsy, Lyft, Ripple...).
3. Warm illustrated scene (a cyclist/street scene) - human, friendly, not a UI.
4. "Recognized by independent analyst firms" - Gartner / Forrester / Liminal badges as colored cards.
5. Dark section: "Introducing a better way to balance trust and privacy" + a mock sign-up UI ("Verify who you are while keeping your online activity private").
6. "Building blocks for your ideal identity solution" - modular platform pitch.
7. "We protect people, not just data."
8. "Security and privacy at our core."
9. "Ready to get started?" closing CTA on lavender.
10. Footer.

## Branding
- **Color:** Light gray/off-white base with a signature periwinkle/lavender, plus warm illustration palettes (ochre, teal). Friendly and human, not sterile-technical.
- **Typography:** Very large, thin-ish grotesque headline in near-black; calm gray body. The oversized "Keeping the internet human" is the whole design.
- **Logo/mark:** Purple asterisk/spark glyph + "persona" lowercase wordmark.
- **Tone:** Humane, reassuring, trustworthy. Reframes a compliance product as a people product.

## UX / patterns worth borrowing
- **Mission-level plain headline + literal subhead** - the single best clarity move in the set for a hard technical product. This is the pattern babit should study most.
- Analyst-badge cards as trust proof for an abstract security product.
- Illustration of ordinary people/places to make an invisible backend feel human.
- "We protect people, not just data" reframes the value in human terms.

## What NOT to borrow
- "Keeping the internet human" alone is abstract; it only works because the subhead immediately grounds it. A headline this poetic without a literal follow-up would fail. babit must always pair mission-line with a concrete "what it does."
- Gartner/Forrester badges require you to actually have them.
