# Ramp

- **URL:** https://ramp.com
- **Screenshots viewed:** `shots/ramp-hero.png`, `shots/ramp.png`

## One plain sentence
One place for a company's cards, expenses, bill payments, and banking, with AI doing the finance busywork.

## The hero
- **Headline:** "Time is money. Save both."
- **Subhead:** "Cards, expenses, bill payments, and banking* - in the blink of AI."
- **CTA:** Inline "What's your work email?" field + "Get started for free" (bright lime-yellow button). A top promo bar: "New: AI Token Spend Management - see, understand, and control your AI bill." A small stat above the headline: "US CORPORATE PAYMENTS PROCESSED BY RAMP: 0.8571864%" (a live-feeling counter).
- **Why it lands:** "Time is money. Save both." is a familiar idiom flipped into a promise - instantly legible to anyone, zero jargon. The subhead lists the four concrete things in plain nouns. "in the blink of AI" is a cute pun on "blink of an eye" (borderline cutesy but clear). Strong layman clarity.

## Landing structure (from full-page shot)
1. Hero + tilted product screenshots (Reporting dashboard, Bills, a Ramp card).
2. A live stats ticker bar: "AGENTS AT WORK TODAY" - fields coded, agent interactions, expenses reviewed, spend allocated, invoices processed, violations classified (real-time counters as proof of activity).
3. "Join 70,000 of the world's most ambitious companies... 3.2x faster" + customer logos + a "$1M+" saved testimonial video card.
4. "One platform for all of finance. Agents for every workflow, working 24/7." - product tile grid (Cards & Expenses, Procure to pay, Accounting automation, Banking, Cash & Treasury, Integrations).
5. "Systems that never spoke" - integrations visual.
6. "Built on the intelligence of 70k+ finance teams. One platform for the agentic era."
7. Customer stories (Perplexity, Gilbane) with product UI.
8. "Scale the team. Shrink the paperwork." / "We've got the receipts." closing.
9. Dark footer.

## Branding
- **Color:** Clean white/light-gray base, near-black text, one electric lime-yellow accent for CTAs and highlights. Dot-grid texture in product areas. Crisp and modern.
- **Typography:** Big bold grotesque headline, very tight and punchy; small-caps utility labels for the stat rows.
- **Logo/mark:** "ramp" lowercase wordmark with a small upward swoosh.
- **Tone:** Efficient, confident, slightly playful. Data-forward (live counters) as proof of a working machine.

## UX / patterns worth borrowing
- **Live activity counters** ("AGENTS AT WORK TODAY: expenses reviewed 362,259, violations classified...") make an invisible automated system feel real and trustworthy. Highly relevant to babit, which records agent activity - a live "receipts issued / actions verified" ticker would resonate.
- Idiom-based headline that a child could parse.
- "We've got the receipts" - literal, plain-English trust framing (also directly on-theme for babit).
- Concrete outcome numbers (3.2x faster, $1M+, 70,000 companies).
- Single bright accent color used sparingly for all actions.

## What NOT to borrow
- The tiny "0.8571864%" processed stat is clever but cryptic to a layman - decoration over communication.
- "in the blink of AI" risks aging badly / feels like a forced pun.
- Dense tile grids of finance jargon (Procure to pay, Cash & Treasury) lose non-finance readers.
