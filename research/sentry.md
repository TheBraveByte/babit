# Sentry

- **URL:** https://sentry.io
- **Screenshots viewed:** `shots/sentry-hero.png`, `shots/sentry.png`

## One plain sentence
Software that watches your app in production, tells you when it breaks, and helps you find and fix the cause fast.

## The hero
- **Headline:** "Code breaks, fix it faster" ("breaks," set in bright pink, tilted, for emphasis)
- **Subhead:** "Application monitoring software considered \"not bad\" by millions of developers."
- **CTAs:** "GET STARTED" (white) + "GET DEMO" (outline). Pill banner above: "Sentry MCP: fix production issues right from your LLM." A cheeky "Marketing Mode: ON" toggle sits top-right. Below the fold: labeled product cards (Errors, Replays, Logs, Traces, Seer/Root Cause) with a purple robot mascot.
- **Why it lands:** "Code breaks, fix it faster" is blunt and true - it names the pain (things break) and the promise (fix faster) in four words. The self-deprecating "not bad" subhead is disarming and memorable. For developers it is perfectly clear; for a pure layman "application monitoring" and "MCP/LLM" are jargon, but the core sentence still communicates "when your thing breaks, this helps."

## Landing structure (from full-page shot)

10. Footer.

## Branding
- **Color:** Deep purple/violet canvas, hot-pink and lime-green accents, playful. Dark, energetic, distinctly developer-culture.
- **Typography:** Heavy rounded-ish bold sans for headlines with expressive color/rotation on key words; readable body.
- **Logo/mark:** Chevron/mountain glyph + "SENTRY" caps.
- **Tone:** Irreverent, confident, developer-native ("not bad," "Marketing Mode: ON," robot mascot). Personality-forward.

## UX / patterns worth borrowing
- **Pain-then-promise headline** in the fewest possible words ("Code breaks, fix it faster") - a proven formula babit can adapt (name the fear, then the fix).
- "Go from Issue -> Context -> Fix" is a tiny 3-step arrow flow that explains the product loop at a glance - close cousin to babit's AUTHORITY -> ACTION -> EVIDENCE -> VERIFICATION.
- Labeled product cards so you see the pieces (Errors/Logs/Traces) without reading.
- Honest personality (self-deprecating copy) builds trust and stands out.
- Security/compliance badges near the bottom for credibility.

## What NOT to borrow
- Heavy inside-joke voice ("Marketing Mode: ON," "not bad," mascot) assumes an in-group; a layman may find it unserious for an accountability product.
- Dense jargon (MCP, LLM, spans, traces, profiles) with no plain-language gloss.
- Busy purple collage can feel noisy; hierarchy competes with decoration.
