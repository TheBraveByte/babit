# Vanta

- **URL:** https://www.vanta.com
- **Screenshots viewed:** `shots/vanta-hero.png`, `shots/vanta.png`

## One plain sentence
Software that automatically gets your company security-certified and continuously proves you stay compliant.

## The hero
- **Headline:** "Trust is everything"
- **Subhead:** "Earn and prove it with 35+ compliance frameworks, automated and continuously monitored."
- **CTA:** Inline "nodrama@llama.com" email field + "Get a demo" (purple pill). Promo bar above: "Vanta Delivers: See how Clay and Hard Rock Digital run GRC with AI tools." Illustrated llama mascot draped in compliance-badge medals (SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, GDPR). Below: a product screenshot (automated evidence collection, ISO 42001 progress) and a customer logo strip (Ramp, Cursor, Snowflake, Clay, Lovable, Samsara).
- **Why it lands:** "Trust is everything" is a three-word, universally understood truth - perfect emotional entry for an otherwise dry category (GRC/compliance automation). The subhead's verbs "Earn and prove it" are plain and map exactly to what the product does. The mascot literally wears the certifications as medals - an instantly readable visual metaphor. Strong clarity for the value even if "compliance frameworks" is slightly technical.

## Branding
- **Color:** Signature soft lavender/periwinkle hero, white body, deep-purple accents and footer. Friendly, clean, a touch editorial.
- **Typography:** Elegant high-contrast serif headline ("Trust is everything") - unusual for a security tool, reads premium and human; calm sans body.
- **Logo/mark:** "Vanta" wordmark; the llama is the brand character.
- **Tone:** Reassuring, warm, lightly playful ("nodrama@llama.com," medal llama) while still credible.

## UX / patterns worth borrowing
- **Three-word emotional truth headline + literal "what we do" subhead** - same winning pattern as Persona; babit should emulate.
- **Visual metaphor for an abstract value:** the llama wearing certification medals makes "proof of compliance" concrete and memorable. babit could do similar for "receipts/evidence."
- Verbs that ARE the product ("Earn and prove it").
- Product screenshot showing the actual evidence/audit view right under the hero (proof it's real).
- Warm serif headline to make a technical category feel human.

## What NOT to borrow
- "GRC," "compliance frameworks," "AI Agentic Trust Platform" are still insider terms; Vanta gets away with it because buyers know the pain. babit's layman would need these unpacked.
- Mascot whimsy must not undercut seriousness for an accountability/evidence product - use sparingly.
