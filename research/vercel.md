# Vercel

- **URL:** https://vercel.com
- **Screenshots viewed:** `shots/vercel-hero.png`, `shots/vercel.png`

## One plain sentence
The cloud platform where you build, deploy, and run web apps and AI agents.

## The hero
- **Headline:** "Agentic Infrastructure"
- **Right-column tagline set:** "For coding agents / To ship apps and agents / Automated by agents"
- **CTAs:** "Deploy now" (black pill) + "Talk to sales" (white pill). A small top pointer: "Vercel Agent works where you do - Add to Slack ->". Center visual: an abstract glowing triangular constellation of dots (the Vercel mark, animated-feeling). Logo row below: BLACKBOX.AI, Charles Schwab, DoorDash, OpenAI, Supreme, The Weather Company, Polymarket.
- **Why it lands (weakly for a layman):** "Agentic Infrastructure" is pure insider jargon - two abstract words with no verb and no benefit. A non-technical reader learns nothing about what happens or why they'd care. The three right-column phrases are also all "agents." This hero optimizes for brand minimalism and an in-crowd audience, not for comprehension.

## Landing structure (from full-page shot)
1. Hero (two-word headline + abstract mark + logo row).
2. "Build agents on infrastructure that thinks like them" - Notion AI-chat example, "Notion powers millions of agent conversations daily on Vercel."
3. "Ship apps that scale from zero to millions instantly" - Zapier example UI, "Your tools. Your rules. Any AI."
4. (Page is short / rest is whitespace in capture.)

## Branding
- **Color:** Near-monochrome - white/light-gray canvas, black text, grayscale logos and mark. Extreme minimalism; almost no color.
- **Typography:** Large clean geometric sans (Geist), tight, confident, lots of negative space.
- **Logo/mark:** The black triangle; echoed as the glowing dot-constellation centerpiece.
- **Tone:** Austere, elite, design-forward. Assumes you already know Vercel.

## UX / patterns worth borrowing
- Immense whitespace and a single abstract brand visual can feel premium and calm.
- Real customer examples embedded as product UIs (Notion, Zapier) to show the platform in use.
- Strong logo row immediately under the hero.
- "Your tools. Your rules. Any AI." (on the Zapier card) is a crisp, plain, ownership-framed line - the opposite of the hero's abstraction, and a better model for babit.

## What NOT to borrow
- **"Agentic Infrastructure" is the anti-pattern for babit** - a two-word abstract noun phrase with no verb, no benefit, no plain meaning. This is exactly the "too technical, a layman won't understand" trap babit must avoid.
- Monochrome minimalism only works when the brand is already famous; a new product needs more explanation and warmth.
- No plain-language "what is this" anywhere near the top.
