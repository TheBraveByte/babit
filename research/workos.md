# WorkOS

- URL: https://workos.com
- Screenshots viewed: workos-hero.png (above-the-fold), workos.png (full page)

## What it does (as the hero says it)
Lets your app sell to big enterprise customers by adding the features they demand (SSO, SCIM, audit logs) in a few lines of code.

## Hero
- Eyebrow: "Introducing Atlas, your AI coworker ->"
- Headline: "Your app, Enterprise Ready." — first line black, "Enterprise Ready." in a purple-to-blue gradient.
- Subhead: "Start selling to enterprise customers with just a few lines of code. Implement features like single sign-on in minutes instead of months."
- CTAs: "Get started" (solid purple pill) + "Talk to an expert ->".
- Why it lands for a layman: the headline states the OUTCOME (your app becomes enterprise-ready), not the mechanism. The subhead is concrete and uses a time contrast anyone understands ("minutes instead of months"). No jargon in the first sentence.

## Landing structure (visible)
1. Nav: logo left; Products / Docs / Customers / Resources / Pricing; "Sign in" outline pill right.
2. Hero: headline + subhead + CTAs on the left; a real product surface on the right — a stacked list of feature toggles (Connectors, Bot Blocking [Enabled], Agent Auth [Enabled], Audit Logs [Ready], Enterprise SSO [Ready], SCIM [Ready], RBAC) with green status badges. It shows the actual product, not an illustration.
3. "TRUSTED BY" logo wall: OpenAI, Anthropic, Cursor, Perplexity, Thinking Machines, fal, Sierra, Baseten, Parallel, Replit, bolt, clay, Carta, Synthesia, Indeed — real, recognizable names.

## Branding
- Near-white background, near-black headline text, one purple accent (with a purple-blue gradient used only on the headline emphasis and CTA).
- Typography: large, bold grotesque headline; clean sans body. Confident scale.
- Logo: geometric purple mark + wordmark.
- Tone: credible, enterprise, calm. The product surface itself is the visual.

## Borrow
- Outcome-first headline; the mechanism comes later.
- A subhead with a concrete, human time/benefit contrast.
- Show the REAL product as the hero visual — a status list with toggles/badges reads instantly as "this is a real tool."
- Honest social proof via a clean, recognizable logo wall (only if real).
- One accent color used sparingly for emphasis + CTA.

## Do not borrow
- The AI-coworker eyebrow ("Atlas") — off-concept for babit; do not bolt an AI-buzzword banner on top.
- The gradient on the headline is fine once, but do not gradient everything.
- Do not fabricate a logo wall — babit has no customers yet; use a different trust device (open verification, the receipt itself).
